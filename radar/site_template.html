How far to trust this

I labeled 40 postings by hand and scored both methods on them. On AI product work, Jev got 15 of 15 and a title rule got 5 of 15. On whether a role is really PM, Jev got 40 of 40 and the title rule got 39 of 40.

That's a small sample: the real error rate could be as high as about 20%. Those scores were measured on jev-1.13.0 on September 29, 2026, and this page runs on the latest version. The numbers next to each answer are Jev's own confidence, and anything below its floor goes to Needs review.
